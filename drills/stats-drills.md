# Statistics & Probability Drills

Foundational probability rules, hypothesis testing protocols, and sample size formulas.

## 1. Sample Size Formula for Two-Sample T-Test
$$n = \frac{2 \cdot (Z_{\alpha/2} + Z_{\beta})^2 \cdot \sigma^2}{\delta^2}$$
* $Z_{\alpha/2}$: Critical value for significance level $\alpha$ (1.96 for 95% confidence).
* $Z_{\beta}$: Critical value for statistical power $1 - \beta$ (0.84 for 80% power).
* $\sigma^2$: Variance of the population metric.
* $\delta$: Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE).
