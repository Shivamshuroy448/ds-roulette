# Product Analytics Drills

Metric taxonomy, guardrail framework, and experiment interpretation.

## 1. North Star Metric Architecture
A robust North Star Metric must capture:
1. **Value Delivery**: The customer successfully experiences the core utility.
2. **Frequency & Breadth**: Repeated engagement across active cohorts.
3. **Leading Indicator**: Correlates with long-term retention and expansion.

## 2. Guardrail Counter-Metrics
* **Primary Metric**: Search result click-through rate (CTR).
* **Guardrail Metric**: Session abandonment rate without click; bounce back rate within 5 seconds.
