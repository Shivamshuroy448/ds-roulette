## Intuitive Mental Model
Imagine taking a 2D photograph of a 3D sculpture. You want to choose an angle that captures the widest spread and details of the sculpture without flattening it. PCA finds orthogonal directions (eigenvectors of the covariance matrix) that maximize the variance of the data.

---

## The Interview Trap & Senior Insight
Always standardize your features (StandardScaler) before running PCA! If one feature is measured in kilograms (0-100) and another in grams (0-100,000), the variance of the grams feature will completely dominate the first principal component regardless of real importance.

---

## Technical Implementation
```python
from sklearn.decomposition import PCA
from sklearn.preprocessing import StandardScaler

# Step 1: Mandatory Standardization
scaler = StandardScaler()
X_scaled = scaler.fit_transform(X)

# Step 2: Fit PCA & check cumulative explained variance
pca = PCA(n_components=0.95) # Retain 95% of total variance
X_reduced = pca.fit_transform(X_scaled)
print(f"Reduced from {X.shape[1]} to {X_reduced.shape[1]} components.")
```
